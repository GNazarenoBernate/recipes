Milanesa napolitana

Description

Milanesa napolitana is a popular Argentine dish. Original from Buenos Aires in the late 1940s, and besides the name, its origin is not from Naples, Italy. The dish is made by topping a breaded and fried beef cutlet (milanesa) with tomato sauce, ham, and melted cheese, then baking it until golden. It's often served with French fries or a simple salad.

Ingredients

  • Beef cutlets
  • Eggs (2 or more, depends on the amount of beef)
  • Minced garlic and parsley
  • Flour
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Oil (for frying)
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Ham
  • Tomato slices

Steps

  1. Prepare the meat: Clean the thin beef cutlets (top round, eye of round, or sirloin tip) by trimming off any fat or sinew from the edges. If they are too thick, gently pound them with a meat mallet to tenderize them and make the thickness even.
  2. Make the egg wash: Beat the eggs in a wide bowl along with the finely chopped garlic and parsley, plus salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Bread the cutlets: Dip each cutlet into the egg mixture, making sure it is fully coated. Then, press it firmly into the breadcrumbs with the palms of your hands on both sides so the coating sticks well. (Optional: for an extra crispy crust, repeat the egg and breadcrumb steps).
  4. Cook the milanesas:
    1. Fried: Fry them in plenty of hot oil for 1 to 2 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove them and place them on paper towels to drain any excess oil.
    2. Baked: Place them on a lightly oiled baking sheet and bake at high heat for about 10 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Prepare the sauce: While they cook, season the tomato sauce or puree with salt, pepper, a drizzle of olive oil, and oregano (you can use it uncooked or simmer it in a small pot for 5 minutes).
  6. Assemble the toppings: Arrange the cooked milanesas on a baking sheet. Spread a generous layer of tomato sauce over each one, place one or two slices of cooked ham on top, and cover with mozzarella or a good melting cheese.
  7. Melt and serve: Place the baking sheet in the hot oven for 5 to 10 minutes, just until the cheese is completely melted and bubbling. Remove from the oven, sprinkle with dried oregano (and thin slices of fresh tomato if you like), and serve immediately.
