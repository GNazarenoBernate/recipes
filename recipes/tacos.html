Tacos

Description

Tacos are a quintessential Mexican street food consisting of a small, hand-sized corn or wheat tortilla folded or rolled around a savory filling, typically topped with fresh salsas, herbs, and lime. Originating in Mexico long before European arrival—where indigenous peoples used freshly made corn tortillas as edible utensils to scoop up fish, meats, and beans—tacos have become a global phenomenon, often adapted locally in places like Argentina by swapping traditional Mexican cuts for local grill favorites like bife de chorizo, bondiola, or even using chimichurri alongside classic salsa.

Ingredients

  • For the tortillas (or store-bought):
    • Masa harina (nixtamalized corn flour) or regular all-purpose wheat flour (Note: Wheat flour tortillas, known as "rapiditas" in Argentina, are very popular and easy to make at home).
    • Warm water
    • Salt
    • Vegetable oil or lard (if making wheat flour tortillas)
  • For the meat filling:
    • Beef (skirt steak/entraña, flank steak/vacío, or rump roast/nalga cut into small strips or cubes) or chicken breast
    • Onion (sliced into thin strips)
    • Bell peppers (red and green, sliced into strips)
    • Garlic (minced)
    • Spices: Cumin, smoked paprika (pimentón), chili powder (or ají molido for an Argentine touch), oregano, salt, and black pepper
    • Vegetable oil for cooking
  • For the toppings and salsas:
    • Fresh cilantro (coriander) leaves, chopped (or fresh parsley if you prefer a milder, local herb)
    • White or red onion, finely diced
    • Fresh tomatoes, diced
    • Avocado or homemade guacamole
    • Lime wedges (essential for squeezing on top)
    • Shredded cheese or sour cream / cream cheese (optional)

Prerequisites

  • Have a heavy skillet, griddle (plancha), or comal ready for heating the tortillas, plus a large frying pan or wok for the filling.
  • Keep a clean kitchen towel handy to wrap the warm tortillas so they stay soft and pliable.

Steps

  1. Prepare the fresh toppings (Pico de Gallo): Finely dice the tomatoes, onion, and fresh cilantro. Mix them in a small bowl with a pinch of salt and a generous squeeze of fresh lime juice. Set aside so the flavors meld. If using avocado, mash it with lime juice, salt, and diced tomato to make a quick guacamole.
  2. Prep and season the meat: Cut your chosen beef (such as entraña or nalga) or chicken into small, bite-sized strips or cubes so they cook quickly and fit easily inside a tortilla. Toss the meat in a bowl with minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, ají molido (or chili powder), oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Make the tortillas (if making from scratch):
    • For corn tortillas: Mix masa harina, warm water, and a pinch of salt until it forms a smooth, pliable dough. Roll into golf-ball-sized spheres and press them flat between two pieces of plastic wrap using a tortilla press or the bottom of a heavy pan.
    • For wheat tortillas: Mix flour, salt, oil, and warm water, knead for 5 minutes, let rest for 15 minutes, and roll out small balls thinly with a rolling pin.
    • Cook each tortilla on a hot, dry skillet for about 30 to 45 seconds per side until light brown spots appear. Immediately wrap them in a clean towel to trap the steam and keep them soft. (If using store-bought tortillas, simply warm them up on a dry skillet for 15–20 seconds per side).
  4. Sauté the vegetables: Heat a splash of oil in a large frying pan or wok over high heat. Add the sliced onions and bell peppers with a pinch of salt. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until they are tender-crisp and slightly charred at the edges. Remove them from the pan and set aside.
  5. Cook the meat: In the same hot pan, add a little more oil if needed and spread the seasoned meat out in a single layer. Sear over high heat for 4 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meat is browned on the outside and cooked through.
  6. Combine the filling: Return the cooked onions and peppers to the pan with the meat, toss everything together for 1 minute to combine the flavors, and remove from the heat.
  7. Assemble and serve: Take a warm tortilla from the towel, place a spoonful of the hot meat and pepper mixture down the center, and top it with pico de gallo, guacamole, and extra chopped cilantro. Serve immediately with fresh lime wedges on the side to squeeze right before eating.
